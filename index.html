ISA S5.1 Tag Numbers
1st letter = variable, following = function. PIC-101 = Pressure Indicating Controller.
Variables: A Analysis · F Flow · P Pressure · L Level · T Temp · S Speed · V Vibration · Z Position · W Weight · D Density · C Conductivity
Functions: I Indicate · C Control · T Transmit · R Record · A Alarm · S Switch · E Element · Y Relay/Compute
PT-101 transmitter · FE-310 orifice · LSH-402 level switch high · PSHH-501 pressure switch high-high trip
4-20 mA & Signals
4mA=0%(LRV) · 20mA=100%(URV) · loop supply ~24VDC
NAMUR NE43: <3.6mA under-range/fault · >20.5mA over-range
Zero error = shift at LRV (offset). Span error = slope at URV.
2-wire loop-powered · 3-wire · 4-wire separate power/signal
Vibration — ISO 10816 (Group 2)
RMS velocity mm/s (10-1000 Hz):
A <1.4 good · B 1.4-2.8 satisfactory · C 2.8-4.5 unsatisfactory, plan repair · D >4.5 unacceptable
Alarm at B/C, trip at C/D. Verify machine group (ISO 20816 supersedes).
Hazardous Areas (ATEX/IECEx)
Zone 0 continuous · Zone 1 likely · Zone 2 unlikely/brief.
Ex i intrinsic safety (low power, Zone 0/1/2). Ex d flameproof (Zone 1/2).
Zener barrier needs dedicated earth, no isolation. Galvanic isolator = true isolation, kills ground loops.
Marking: Ex ia IIC T4 Ga · Ex d IIC T6 Gb
Orifice Sizing Basics
β=d/D, usable 0.2-0.75. Low β = more ΔP/turndown, high perm loss. High β = less loss, weaker low flow.
Taps: flange (1" from face) · corner · D & D/2 (vena contracta) · pipe taps.
ISO 5167 / ASME MFC-3M for final sizing.
Torque & K-Factor
T=K×D×F. K: lube 0.13-0.16 · light oil 0.18-0.20 · dry 0.20-0.30.
Star/cross pattern, staged 30%→60%→100%. Use ASME PCC-1 / manufacturer chart for real values.
Grounding & Bonding
Gen equip ≤25Ω · critical/substation ≤5Ω or ≤1Ω · IS systems ≤1Ω.
Single-point = avoid ground loops (signal shields). Multi-point = HF noise.
Test: fall-of-potential (3-pt) · clamp-on. Shield grounded at ONE end only.
TC Color Codes & RTD
J White/Red · K Green/Red · T Blue/Red · E Purple/Red · N Orange/Red · S Black/Red
RTD Pt100=100Ω@0°C, α=0.00385 (IEC 60751). 3-wire most common, 4-wire eliminates lead error.
Valve Actuators — Fail Safe
Reverse-acting: air below diaphragm → stem up → opens → ATO → Fail-Closed.
Direct-acting: air above → stem down → closes → ATC → Fail-Open.
Fail position set by actuator + body seating together. ESD fuel valve = FC, vent = FO.
PLC Basics & I/O
Scan cycle · NO/NC · Start/Stop seal-in · Set/Reset · Timers TON/TOF/TP · Counters · Interlocks · Alarms.
DI/DO 24VDC · AI/AO 4-20mA/0-10V. PNP sourcing (+24V out), NPN sinking (0V).
Relay out = AC/DC high current slow. Transistor out = DC fast long-life.

Manufacturer Error Codes

FAIL ELECT (electronics) · Sensor miscompare (ribbon cable) · Zero/Span Fail (re-trim vs ref) · Press/Temp Limit (overrange event).
Yokogawa EJA/EJX
Er.01 CAP MODULE FAULT · self-check menu via BT200 · burst-mode w/ no output = comms/wiring.
ABB TTH300
Sensor break/open (RTD/TC wiring) · Out of range (re-range) · Config/EEPROM error (factory reset then re-verify).
Honeywell Sensepoint XCD
Flashing yellow = fault, output <1mA (check cartridge seating). Flashing red = real alarm (bump test first).

Ground-Loop / Noise Checklist

☐ Shield grounded at ONE end only (control room)
☐ Signal & power cables separated / different trays
☐ No shared neutral/return between loops
☐ Check continuity: shield NOT bonded both ends
☐ Measure mV AC on signal pair — > a few mV indicates coupling
☐ Use galvanic isolator if ground potential differences exist
☐ Verify VFD/motor cables dressed away from instrument runs

🔥 Hot Work / Confined Space Quick Notes

Hot Work: Permit required · gas test <30 min prior · fire watch during + 30-60 min after · remove/cover combustibles · extinguisher present.

Confined Space: Permit + attendant · continuous gas monitoring (O₂ 19.5-23.5%, LEL <10%, toxics) · ventilation · rescue plan · comms maintained.
